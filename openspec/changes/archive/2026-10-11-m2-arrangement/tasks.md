# Tasks

M2 is built as eight demos on one branch (`change/m2-arrangement`), checked by the PO one at a time, with one PR at the end. Each group ends with a demo script and a PO acceptance task. Build: `scripts/build.ps1` or `cmake --build --preset windows-msvc-debug`; tests: `ctest --preset windows-msvc-debug -LE hardware`. Fixtures are the M1 files in `tests/audio-fixtures/`.
