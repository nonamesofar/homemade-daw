# Tasks

M2b is built as four demos on one branch (`change/m2b-capture`), checked by the PO one at a time, with one PR at the end. Each group ends with a demo script and a PO acceptance task. Build: `scripts/build.ps1` or `cmake --build --preset windows-msvc-debug`; tests: `ctest --preset windows-msvc-debug -LE hardware`. Tests that open a real audio device carry the `hardware` label (CI leaves them out).
